## ChemPal is a tool, not a store

ChemPal shows you listings that already exist on third-party supplier sites and
helps you compare them. It doesn't sell anything, and any purchase you make is
**solely between you and the supplier**, under that supplier's own terms. ChemPal
isn't involved in orders, payment, shipping, quality, or disputes — take those up
with the supplier directly.

## Always verify before you buy

Prices, quantities, availability, purity, and chemical identifiers come **straight
from suppliers and public databases** and can be incomplete, out of date, or wrong.
Currency conversions are estimates. **Confirm every detail on the supplier's own
listing** — and read its Safety Data Sheet — before buying or handling anything.

responsible** for:

- following all laws that apply to buying, possessing, transporting, using, and

- obtaining any licence or permit you need; and
- handling everything safely.

ChemPal doesn't verify whether a product is legal for you to buy or safe for you to
use, and surfacing a listing is not a recommendation. It's an informational tool
